About the data

What is counted

Storms, floods, droughts, heatwaves, cold waves, wildfires, and rain-triggered landslides recorded in EM-DAT since 2000. Earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, and epidemics are excluded. EM-DAT records an event when it meets at least one criterion: 10 or more deaths, 100 or more people affected, a declared state of emergency, or a call for international assistance. An event that affects several countries is counted once for each country.

How to read it

Counts reflect how disasters are reported as well as how often they occur, so large countries and countries with strong reporting systems tend to show more events. Changes in reporting also affect long-term trends. Figures for the current year are provisional and grow as new events are entered.
